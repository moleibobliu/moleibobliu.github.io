ABOUT

Statistics across heterogeneous data.

My research sits at the intersection of statistical theory, machine learning, and biomedical data science.

A central theme is reliable learning across data sources and populations. I study how auxiliary samples, unlabeled observations, surrogates, and structural knowledge can be used efficiently without requiring unrealistic homogeneity or perfectly specified models.

I received my Ph.D. in Biostatistics from Harvard University and subsequently joined Columbia University as a tenure-track Assistant Professor. Since March 2025, I have been a Researcher and tenure-track Assistant Professor at Peking University.
